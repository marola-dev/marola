## Decisions

1. **Tasks 2–7 need only task 1.** Stacked 1 → … → 7 for review order, but `depends on` is the
   real graph: any of them can merge right after task 1. The one shared file is MIP-0042, which
   task 6 edits in §5.3 and task 7 in §3; a restack there is a trivial rebase.
2. **Task 1 carries the dark-only switch.** The five existing diagrams must be re-rendered and
   legible before anything is added on top of them.
3. **Task 7 is last and optional.** It is the only task that adds a container, and the MIP makes
   it conditional on the sketches earning their place.
