## Sequencing notes

- Tasks 1/3 (Bahia) and 2/4 (Rio) are independent of each other — different institutes, different
  files, no shared code beyond whatever parser-sharing decision task 2 makes (flagged there as an
  implementation-time call, not pre-decided). They can be worked in parallel.
- Tasks 5 and 6 are the integration points and each depends on both halves (parser + coordinates)
  for their own institute — not parallel with their own dependencies, but 5 and 6 are parallel with
  *each other* once their prerequisites land.
- The `Risk` row this MIP's own header names — the coordinate table drifting as institutes add or
  retire points, with no self-updating feed to catch it — applies to tasks 3 and 4 specifically.
  Neither task's own tests can catch future drift (that's the nature of a hand-curated table); the
  mitigation is procedural (re-check when `just benchmark` runs against a `rio`/`salvador` origin
  starts returning more "no data" than expected), not something to over-engineer into task 3/4's
  scope now.
