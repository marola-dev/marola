# marola's partners

marola is built in the open, as citizen science, and it does not go alone. These are the
partners that walk with it.

## Leave No Trace

<p><a href="https://lnt.org/"><img src="./docs/assets/sponsors/lnt-proud-partner.png" alt="Leave No Trace, Proud Partner" width="200" /></a></p>

The [Leave No Trace Center for Outdoor Ethics](https://lnt.org/) teaches people to enjoy nature
without leaving a trace, with seven simple principles for anyone outdoors. marola is an official
Proud Partner: whoever goes to the sea should leave it as they found it.

The mark above belongs to Leave No Trace and is not under marola's licence (see
[`REUSE.toml`](./REUSE.toml)).

## OpenDiving

[OpenDiving](https://opendiving.app/) is an open source logbook for scuba divers, "a dive log built
to outlive every vendor": it imports dive-computer files, groups dives into trips and can be
self-hosted ([github.com/opendiving](https://github.com/opendiving)). Both projects are built on
open data, and they link to each other: OpenDiving lists marola among its partner apps
([opendiving/opendiving#106](https://github.com/opendiving/opendiving/issues/106)).
