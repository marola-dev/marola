# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include: demonstrating empathy,
being respectful of differing opinions, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback, and
focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include: sexualized language or imagery, trolling and insulting
comments, public or private harassment, publishing others' private information without permission,
and other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities and scope

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and are expected to take appropriate
corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior. This applies within all project spaces
(issues, PRs, discussions) and when an individual is officially representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

This repo has no dedicated conduct email. Report an incident by opening a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/h0ffmann/marola/issues/new) marked confidential where possible,
or by contacting the maintainer ([@h0ffmann](https://github.com/h0ffmann)) directly via GitHub. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
